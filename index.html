Our story

Named for the royal palm.

In the Dominican Republic, the yagua is the broad sheath of the royal palm, the tall palm that shades the north coast and once roofed the island's country homes. Casa Yagua carries that spirit: shade, sea breeze and an easy, barefoot pace.
